# How to edit the questionnaire questions (staff guide)

This guide explains how to log in to the Will Tool as staff and change the questions that clients see when they fill in the form. You don’t need any technical setup—just the same link you use for the Will Tool and your login details.

---

## Before you start

- You need the **web address** where the Will Tool lives (for example the link you send to clients, or the one on your website).
- You need your **staff login** (email and password). If you’re not sure what these are, ask your administrator.
- Use any normal web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, etc.) on a computer or tablet.

---

## Step 1: Open the Will Tool

- Open your browser and go to the Will Tool address (the same one clients use to fill in the form).
- You’ll see the main Will Tool page with the client form and the Aristone logo at the top.

---

## Step 2: Open the staff login

- Look at the **top right** of the page.
- You’ll see a button that says **“Solicitor login”** (it has a small lock icon next to it).
- **Click** that button.

You’ll be taken to a sign-in page. This is separate from the client form—only staff use this.

---

## Step 3: Sign in

- Enter your **email address** (the one set up for you as staff).
- Enter your **password**.
- Click the button to sign in (e.g. “Sign in” or “Log in”).

If something goes wrong (for example “Wrong password” or “User not found”), double-check your email and password. If it still doesn’t work, your administrator may need to check that your account is set up correctly.

---

## Step 4: You’re in the staff area

- After you sign in, you’ll see the **Aristone Solicitors Workspace**.
- At the top you’ll see a dark header with your name and a “Sign out” option.
- Below that you’ll see the main area. By default you’ll be on the **Matters** screen, which lists client matters.

You’re now logged in as staff. The next step is to open the questionnaire editor.

---

## Step 5: Go to “Edit questionnaire”

- Just **below the dark header**, you’ll see two clickable tabs or links side by side:
  - **Matters** – shows the list of client matters.
  - **Edit questionnaire** – lets you change the questions clients see.
- **Click “Edit questionnaire”.**

The page will change to show the questionnaire editor. You’ll see sections (groups of questions) and, inside each section, the actual questions.

---

## Step 6: Change the questions and save

- Each **section** has a title (for example “Personal details” or “Executors”). Click a section to **expand** it if it’s collapsed.
- Inside each section you’ll see the **questions**. You can change the wording of the question text and, where relevant, the answer options (e.g. dropdown choices).
- Edit whatever you need.
- When you’re happy with your changes, go to the **top of the questionnaire editor** and click the **“Save questionnaire”** button.

After you save, the updated questions are live. The next time a client opens or refreshes the form, they’ll see the new wording. You don’t need to do anything else for the changes to appear.

---

## Quick recap

1. Open the Will Tool in your browser.  
2. Click **“Solicitor login”** (top right).  
3. Sign in with your email and password.  
4. Click **“Edit questionnaire”** (the link next to “Matters”).  
5. Expand a section, edit the questions, then click **“Save questionnaire”** at the top.

That’s the full path from opening the tool to saving your edits.

---

## If something doesn’t look right

- **I don’t see “Solicitor login”.**  
  Make sure you’re on the main Will Tool page (where clients fill in the form), not a different website. Try opening the Will Tool link again in a new tab.

- **I don’t see “Edit questionnaire” after I sign in.**  
  You should see it next to “Matters” under the dark header. If it’s missing, you may be on a different screen—click “Matters” first to get to the main staff area, then look for “Edit questionnaire” again. If it’s still not there, your administrator or developer may need to check your access or the app setup.

- **I edited a question but clients still see the old text.**  
  Make sure you clicked **“Save questionnaire”** after editing. If you did and the form still shows old text, ask the client to refresh the page; sometimes the browser shows a cached version.

If you’re still stuck, note what you see on the screen (or take a screenshot) and contact your administrator or the person who looks after the Will Tool for your firm.
