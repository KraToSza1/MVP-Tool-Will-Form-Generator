## 1. Revocation (new clause 1, straight after "This is the Will of …")

Because the Will's *Foreign Assets* clause keeps foreign Wills alive, the revocation is limited to England and Wales:

> **1. Revocation**
> I REVOKE all former Wills and testamentary dispositions made by me so far as they relate to my property in England and Wales.

If the client has **no** foreign assets / foreign Wills, the full form would be used instead:

> I REVOKE all former Wills and testamentary dispositions made by me.

**Question for Mariyam:** use the limited form only when the client answered "Yes" to foreign assets, and the full form otherwise?

## 2. Residuary gift (start of the *Estate Administration / Residue* section)

The section currently has the supporting clauses (failure of shares, IHT, charity, FLIT trustees) but never actually gives the residue.

**a) Residue in shares** (client chose "as shares"):

> I GIVE all my estate not otherwise effectively disposed of by this Will (my "Residuary Estate") to my Trustees to hold on trust to pay my debts, funeral and testamentary expenses and any inheritance tax payable on my death, and to hold what remains for the following beneficiaries in the shares stated:
> [beneficiary name (relationship)] – [share]%; …

**b) Residue into a Flexible Life Interest Trust** (client chose "into a FLIT"):

> I GIVE my Residuary Estate to my Trustees to hold on the terms of the Flexible Life Interest Trust set out in Schedule [n], with [life tenant name (relationship)] as the life tenant and [default beneficiaries] as the beneficiaries after the life tenant's death.
